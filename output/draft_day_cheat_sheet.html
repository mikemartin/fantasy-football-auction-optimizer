DRAFT DAY — auction cheat sheet v2

Sun 30 Aug · $200 · 16 players · you nominate 3rd of 10

Start here

You cannot hold anyone up. People call out numbers. The auctioneer counts down and says sold. No clock on you, no button to press. Say nothing all night and nothing breaks.

The script — the only five things you say

YOUR TURN“Patrick Mahomes, one dollar.”
TO BID“Twenty two.”
TO PASSNothing.
TO STOPNothing.
IF LOST“Who is that?”

Your turn: nominate someone you do not want

Name a player the room will pay heavily for but the numbers say is overpriced. Someone spends $40–60, you spend nothing, and that money is gone from the room. Nobody bids? You own him for $1 — at a dollar every one of these is a fine buy. Work down, crossing names off:

  1. Josh Allen
  2. CeeDee Lamb

  4. Drake Maye
  5. Patrick Mahomes
  6. Justin Herbert

Changed from v1: Gibbs, McCaffrey, Cook and St. Brown are OFF this list — they are your best values (see right), never hand them to the room.

Nominating 3rd is a gift: your first nominations land while wallets are full and the room is at its hottest — exactly when burn nominations do the most damage. Open with Josh Allen; in a superflex room he can soak up $60+ of someone else’s budget on nomination two or three of the whole night.
